RedneckGDX

Browser port of the libGDX Redneck Rampage source port. You must supply your own redneck.grp (from a legal copy of the game) — it is not distributed here.

This container ships a scaffold build. The TeaVM libGDX compile of the game is not complete yet, so no app.js is served. Your redneck.grp is stored in your browser's IndexedDB and will be picked up automatically once a container is deployed with the compiled game. Progress and remaining work is tracked in PATCHES.md in the repo.
